The Painted Arboretum

You have $100 and an empty meadow. Buy trees from the nursery and tap the ground to plant them. Each visitor buys a $25 ticket.

Trees grow while you play, and the seasons turn slowly; each one lasts an hour. Keep an eye out for the gray cat, who wanders by every five minutes.

Use the joystick in the corner to circle the garden, or drag the scene. Pinch or use + and − to zoom.
